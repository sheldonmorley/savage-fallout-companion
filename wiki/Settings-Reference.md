# Settings Reference

All in **Game Settings > Configure Settings > Savage Fallout: Fallout Companion**.

| Setting | Who | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fallout sheet theme** | World | On | Vault-Tec blue-and-yellow styling on character and NPC sheets. |
| **Whisper scavenging results** | World | Off | Each search's chat card goes only to the GM and the player who searched. |
| **Pip-Boy frame folder** | World | The module's frame | The folder holding `pipboy-frame.webp` and `knob-lit.webp`. Point it at your own art to swap the frame, or clear it for a plain screen with tabs along the top. |
| **Pip-Boy quests folder** | World | `Quests` | Journal folder listed under DATA > QUESTS. A *Completed* subfolder marks quests done. |
| **Pip-Boy notes folder** | World | `Notes` | Journal folder listed under DATA > NOTES. |
| **Pip-Boy world map** | World | None | The scene shown under DATA > MAP > WORLD, with its map pins as markers. |
| **Pip-Boy radio folder** | World | `Radio` | Playlist folder whose playlists are radio stations, alongside Fallout Radio. |
| **Pip-Boy sounds** | Each player | On | Clicks and beeps on the Pip-Boy, at your interface volume. |

The radio's volume is remembered per player from the slider on the Pip-Boy.
