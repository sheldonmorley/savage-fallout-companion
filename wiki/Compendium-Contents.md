# Compendium Contents

| Compendium | What's in it |
|---|---|
| Fallout Journals | The book, one journal per chapter, plus the Foundry guides (*02a. Pip-Boy Sheet*, *12a. Workbench*, *12b. Scavenging*). |
| Fallout Ancestries / Ancestral Abilities | Human, ghoul, super mutant, Gen 3 synth and Mister Handy, with their abilities. |
| Fallout Skills | The Fallout skill list. |
| Fallout Edges / Hindrances | Including the converted Perks and the salvage and crafting Edges. |
| Fallout Special Abilities | Abilities used by adversaries and robots. |
| Fallout Weapons / Armor | Weapons, armor and power armor, with their art. |
| Fallout Weapon Mods / Armor Mods / Robot Mods | Mods you install at the Workbench. |
| Fallout Consumables | Chems, food and drink, with their effects. |
| Fallout Equipment | Gear, tools and trade goods. |
| Fallout Crafting Materials | Resources (Common, Uncommon, Rare), Junk, chem ingredients, and the **Rare Recipes** folder of found recipes. |
| Fallout Adversaries | The bestiary, ready to drop on a map, with tokens. |
| Fallout Rollable Tables | Scavenging loot tables, Jackpot, Complications, Crafting Mishap, encounters, mutations, Vault tables and more. |
| Fallout Scenes | Two landing-page scenes. |
| Fallout Playlists | Fallout Radio. |
| Fallout Macros | See [[Macros and API]]. |
| Fallout Workbench Stations | The six benches to place on a map. |
| Fallout Scavenge Nodes | The 20 stock scavenging locations as tokens. |
