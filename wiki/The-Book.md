# The Book

The book is the whole game for play at the table: no VTT needed, just SWADE, dice and cards. It's in the [`docs/`](https://github.com/sheldonmorley/savage-fallout-companion/tree/main/docs) folder as a PDF and as a Word file you can open in Google Docs. The book sticks to table play; notes that only matter in Foundry live in the module's journals.

The module carries the same text as journals (**Fallout Journals** compendium), one per chapter.

## Chapters

1. **Introduction and Setting Rules**: the tone, and the Setting Rules the conversion uses.
2. **Character Creation**: building a wastelander, plus the Pip-Boy.
3. **Ancestries**: human, ghoul, super mutant, Gen 3 synth and Mister Handy.
4. **Backgrounds**
5. **Hindrances**
6. **Skills**: the Fallout skill list.
7. **Edges**: SWADE Edges adjusted for the setting, new Edges, and converted Perks.
8. **Weapons**
9. **Armor and Power Armor**
10. **Chems, Food and Drink**: effects, addiction, the chem cocktail limit.
11. **Equipment**
12. **Scavenging and Crafting**: searching with loot cards, breaking down Junk, workbenches, recipes, mods.
13. **Radiation and Survival**: rads that take up room on the Wound track.
14. **Robots and Companions**
15. **Factions and the Wasteland**
16. **Bestiary**
17. **Game Master's Section**

Appendix: **Changelog**, listing every rules change by version.

## Rules at a glance

- **Radiation** gives rads, which take up Wound boxes but give no penalty. Only RadAway and other treatments remove them. At 2 rads you hit the Breaking Point and may mutate.
- **Scavenging**: a Notice roll; success finds salvage (Junk), each raise draws a loot card, read on the Scavenging Loot tables by suit.
- **Junk** breaks down into Resources (Common, Uncommon, Rare) with no roll: 5 Common and 1 Uncommon per 10 caps, at a workbench or with tools.
- **Crafting** happens at the right bench with a skill roll modified by Complexity. Crafting Edges (Gun Nut, Blacksmith, Armorer, Chemist, Science!, Robotics Expert) unlock their recipes and give +2.
- **Rare recipes** (lost chem formulas, rare feasts, Ballistic Weave and every Science! Masterwork recipe) must be found or taught.
